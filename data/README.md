# Data

`Final_Data.xlsx` has one sheet per country (Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Greece, India, Mexico, NorthKorea, Portugal, Singapore, Spain). Rows are indicators, columns are years 1911–1943 and 1954–2013. MD5: `0db5d958f630a46b2e4d7d9ad640e586`.

Indicators used by the analysis:

| Indicator | Source | Pre-1944 coverage |
|---|---|---|
| GDP per Capita (2011 international $) | Maddison Project Database 2020 | annual |
| Population (thousands) | Clio Infra; for North Korea, Government-General of Korea *Statistical Yearbook* | annual |
| Average Years of Education, Educational Inequality Gini | Clio Infra | 1920, 1930, 1940 (Gini near-annual) |
| Cropland per Capita, SO2 Emissions per Capita | Clio Infra | 1920, 1930, 1940 |
| Total Cattle, Pigs, Goats | Clio Infra | 1920, 1930, 1940 |

## North Korean series

Colonial-period population was obtained by subtracting the southern provinces from peninsula totals in the Government-General yearbook. The 1911–1943 northern GDP per capita series is a regional allocation of peninsula GDP; the allocation steps are not fully documented in the files we inherited. For 1954–1989, growth rates from Cho and Kim (2020, Bank of Korea WP 2020-17) were chained to a Maddison benchmark. The paper discusses these provenance limits in Section 3.1.
